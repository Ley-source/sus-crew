HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, use the joystick.
  • Use: walk to a glowing pad and press E (or the USE button).
  • Goal: finish the tasks. Each one wakes up a hidden crewmate.
  • Investigate: the glowing "?" spots hide clues about the Impostor.
  • Finale: once all four crewmates are found, the Navigation console triggers an emergency meeting.
  • Shortcuts: F fullscreen, M mute, Esc close a window.
